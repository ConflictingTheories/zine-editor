How SCEE Works

Normal encryption: cipher is public, key is secret.
SCEE: the cipher is also secret — encoded inside the key.

What an attacker sees
Ciphertext: uniform random bytes
No cipher identifier

No IV structure
Attack surface: undefined

Plausible deniability for hosts: A server serving the ciphertext envelope knows nothing about its cipher, its contents, or how to decrypt it. They serve a dumb blob. The decryption engine lives in the embed script.
